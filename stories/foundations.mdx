# Foundations

NOTA. is a dark-only, monochrome, space-inspired apparel store. A near-black
void is the canvas, a near-white foreground is the only UI accent, and a single
electric `signal` blue appears only as light (focus, glow, 3D). **Jost** is the
only typeface. Structure comes from thin rules and the `✦` star, not shadows.

This page is the rendered counterpart of `DESIGN.md` (repo root). **If the two
ever disagree**, `DESIGN.md` is the source of truth; **fix this page to match it**.

## Shapes, elevation & motion

- Radius: capsules (`rounded-full`) for buttons/inputs/badges, `rounded-xl` for
  cards and dialogs, `rounded-lg` for tooltips/popovers.
- Elevation: no `shadow-*`. Layers separate with tint, a 1px `border` hairline
  and `backdrop-blur`; glow (in `signal`) is reserved for focus.
- Motion (GSAP): 0.3s / 0.6s / 1.2s, `power3.out` by default, no bounce or
  elastic eases, glitch as the signature transition, and every timeline
  respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

See `DESIGN.md` for the full rules, 3D guidelines and do's and don'ts.
